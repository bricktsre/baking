## Ingredients
- 1 C Unsalted Butter (Room Temperature)
- 270g Sugar
- 370g Flour (Sifted)
- 2 Eggs (Room Temperature)
- 2 tsp Cream of Tartar
- 1 tsp Baking Soda
- 1/2 tsp Kosher Salt
- 1 tsp Cinnamon
- Extra Sugar and Cinnamon for Rolling

## Steps
1. Beat butter till creamy, add sugar and beat till smooth and creamy
2. Add one egg at a time, beating well after each adddition. Continue beating until light and fluffy. Beat in vanilla
3. Mix flour, cream of tarter, baking soda, salt, and cinnamon together (can be done before step 1). Blend into beaten mixture, do not over mix
4. Chill 30 minutes to an hour
5. Scoop into 30g balls (1.6Tb or #40 cookie scoop)
6. Chill for 24-48 hours
7. Pre-heat oven to 400&degF
8. Make a mixture with a ratio of 1Tb sugar to 1 tsp cinnamon. Roll cookies till coated
9. Bake for about 9 minutes (8-10) until edges are set
10. Rest cookies for 5 minutes on sheets before removing to wire racks to cool completely
