# Oatmeal Chocolate Chip Cookies
***
## Ingredients
- 1 C Unsalted Butter (room temperature)
- 100g Sugar
- 200g Brown Sugar
- 200g Oatmeal
- 250g Flour
- 2 Eggs (room temperature)
- 1 tsp Vanilla
- 1 tsp Baking Soda
- 1 tsp Baking Powder
- 1/2 tsp Kosher Salt
- 12oz Chocolate Chips (mini preferred)

## Steps
1. Grind the Oatmeal into a powder, it does not need to be very fine. I find a "Meat Chopper" or "Meat Mallet" works well for this though a metal spoon can do the trick albeit with more effort
2. Beat the butter till creamy, add the sugars and beat till smooth and creamy
3. Beat in eggs and vanilla until light and fluffy
4. Mix flour, ground oatmeal, baking soda, baking powder, and salt together (can be done before step 2). Blend into beaten mixture, do not overmix.
5. Mix in chocolate chips
6. Chill for 1-2 hours
7. Scoop into 30g balls (1.6Tb or #40 cookie scoop)
8. Chill for 24-48 hours
9. Heat oven to 350&degF and bake for about 12-13 minutes until edges are set
10. Rest cookies for 5 minutes on sheets before removing to wire racks to cool completely
